Privacy policy

rclone-backup is used only by its owner, on her own computer, with her own Google account.

  • It requests read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly), solely to download a backup copy of the owner's files to her own storage.
  • No data is sent to, stored by, shared with, or sold to anyone else. There are no servers, analytics, or advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account settings page (Security → Third-party apps).

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
